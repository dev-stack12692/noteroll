Noteroll handbook

Teachers upload class notes. Students join their teacher to read them. Follow these steps to create an account, share material and add marks to a PDF.

Before you start

The PDF limit is 3 MB per file. Teacher notes and PDFs expire after 14 days. Personal pen and highlight marks on PDFs are saved only in that browser. Download or export important copies before they are lost.

For teachers

How to share class notes

  1. Create a teacher account

    Open Teacher sign in. For your first visit, choose New account, enter your name, email and password, then select Create account. If you already have an account, sign in. Use a password with at least 8 characters.

  2. Set up your class

    In Set up your class, enter your class or centre name. Separate batches with commas, for example Class 8, Class 9. UPI details are optional; payment setup is not required to share notes. Tap Create centre.

  3. Add a student and get their code

    Open Students → Add student. Enter the student name and correct Batch, then tap Save. Open that student's edit button to find their 8-letter Join code. Give it only to that student. Each student has a separate code, not one shared code for the batch.

  4. Upload a PDF

    Open Notes → Upload PDF. Enter a title, choose the correct batch or All batches under Who can read it, select your PDF and tap Upload. The PDF must be 3 MB or smaller. It appears in the list when the upload finishes.

  5. Or write a text note

    Choose New note. Enter a title, batch and note text, then tap Publish. This is a teacher's class note, separate from a student's private My notes.

  6. Approve the student's request

    The student creates an account and enters their code. In Students, find Student requests, match the name to the correct student record, then tap Approve. Do not approve unknown or incorrect requests. Ask the student to reload the page and check again.

Wrong file or student?

The delete button in the notes list removes a file for the whole class. A student's Delete everything can also remove their attendance, fees and results. Do not delete records just to test access. Read irreversible-action confirmations carefully.

For students

How to read your teacher's notes

  1. Create a student account

    Open Student sign in. Choose New account and enter your name, email and password. Choose a Student account, not Teacher. If you already have an account, sign in.

  2. Enter your teacher's code

    Under Join your teacher's batch, enter the 8-letter code your teacher gave you and your name. Tap Send request. If you have no code, ask your teacher for the code from your student record.

  3. Wait for approval

    Waiting for the teacher means your request has not been approved yet. Ask your teacher to approve it, then reload the page. Entering a code alone does not unlock the material.

  4. Open a file from Notes and PDFs

    After approval, find Notes and PDFs inside your class card. Tap Open beside a file. You will see material shared with your batch or All batches.

  5. Read, mark and export your copy

    The PDF reader tools are explained below. Use Download original to keep the original file. Use Export marked PDF to keep a copy with your pen and highlight marks.

Inside a PDF

Reader tools

The reader may take a little longer to load the first time you open a PDF. Wait for the page to appear.

Read

Scroll through the page normally. Switch back to Read when you want to scroll after adding marks.

Previous / Next

Open the previous or next PDF page. The current page and total page count appear between the controls.

Zoom

Choose Fit width, 150% or 200%. A zoomed page may need horizontal scrolling.

Pen / Highlight

Select a tool, then drag on the page with your finger, stylus or mouse. Use Ink to change the colour.

Text

Select Text, tap a position on the page and type. Each text mark can contain up to 160 characters.

Eraser

Tap near one of your marks to remove it. This does not erase the original PDF's content.

Undo / Redo

Undo or restore your most recent mark change in the current reader session.

Export marked PDF

Download a PDF with your marks. Download original gives you the teacher's unmarked file.

PDF marks and account notes are different

PDF marks are browser-local. Signing in on another phone or browser will not bring those marks across. Clearing browser data can remove them. Export your marked PDF to keep a copy.

Your own writing

How to use My notes

  1. Write your own note

    On the student home screen, open My notes → New note. Enter a title and text, then tap Save to my account. These text notes are saved in your account, unlike browser-only PDF marks.

  2. Edit or download

    Tap Open on a note. Use Edit to change it or Download to get a text file. Keep backups of important notes.

  3. Sharing is optional

    If Share this note with my teacher is shown, selecting it allows your teacher to read the note. Leave it off to keep the note private to your account. A shared copy appears under the teacher's Notes shared by students. If a sharing error appears after saving, check again.

My notes supports up to 100 text notes. This handbook covers the current main app, not the older Classic notebook workflow.

Optional

Where are the other class tools?

Notes come first. Teachers can find attendance, fees, test results, homework, online exams and settings under Class admin and More. Desktop users also have direct sidebar links.

  • Students: add or edit students, manage batches and review join requests.
  • Attendance / Fees: the teacher's class records. UPI payment setup is not required to read notes.
  • Homework / Results / Exams: students see what their teacher publishes inside their class.
  • Fees: UPI payments go directly to the teacher. I have paid reports a payment claim, not proof of payment; the teacher verifies it.
Keep your work safe

Saving, expiry and privacy

  • Class notes and PDFs expire after 14 days. They are hidden from students; expired material is erased when the app is opened later. Download an original or marked copy before expiry.
  • PDF marks stay in this browser only. Check the auto-save status. If a storage-full error appears, export before closing. Browser-local saving is not cloud backup.
  • My notes is saved in your account. Student text notes are separate from PDF marks. Check the shared flag and your teacher-sharing choice.
  • Main-app PDFs are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not upload sensitive personal documents. Student access depends on teacher approval and batch.
  • AI is not enabled. The main-app reader does not currently provide an AI answer or explain feature.
  • The free service has limits. Heavy use or connection problems can interrupt loading. Keep a backup of important material.

When something gets stuck

Problems & fixes

I cannot find my code, or I see "code not found"

Ask your teacher to open your edit button in Students and give you the Join code. Copy all 8 letters carefully. If a new code was generated, the old one will no longer work. Send the request from a Student account.

I still see Waiting for the teacher after approval

Ask your teacher to confirm that the correct request was approved. Reload the page, or sign out and sign in with the same student email. Do not keep creating new accounts.

The notes list is empty

Did the teacher choose the correct batch or All batches? Is the Batch on your student record correct? Is the material more than 14 days old? Ask your teacher to check these details.

My PDF will not upload

Select a PDF file of 3 MB or smaller. Compress or split larger PDFs before uploading. Check your internet connection and wait for confirmation or for the item to appear in the list.

The PDF takes a long time to load

The reader needs to load its supporting files the first time. Keep a stable internet connection and wait a little. If an error appears, you can keep the file using Download original, then reload the app and try again later.

My pen or highlight marks are missing on another phone

This is expected: PDF marks are browser-local. On the first device, use Export marked PDF, then open that exported file on the other device. Signing in alone does not sync PDF marks.

I draw on the page when I try to scroll

Select Read in the toolbar. Pen and Highlight modes use touch gestures for drawing.

I forgot my password

Enter your email on the sign-in page and tap Forgot password. Check that account's reset email, including the spam folder. Never share your password or reset link.
